What Is a PTE Exam Voucher?

A PTE exam voucher is a prepaid digital voucher or code that can be used toward an eligible PTE examination booking. Instead of paying the applicable exam fee entirely through the standard payment method, you can enter the voucher details during the relevant stage of the booking process.

The voucher has its own validity period, terms of use, redemption conditions, and applicable restrictions. These details can vary depending on the voucher, so it is important to review the specific product information before purchasing.
